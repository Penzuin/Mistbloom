# Halloween Festival | 38 Halloween Furniture Pieces

**Price:** $20 (38 pieces with animated ones, rounded to the nearest $10 per the pricing rule)

---

Pumpkins, a witch's corner, a graveyard and the critters that live there.

**Halloween Festival** is a Halloween furniture set from Mistbloom Studios, with everything you need for a spooky autumn market on your server.

The jack-o'-lanterns, lamp posts and candles give off real light, and 12 pieces are animated: flickering flames, bubbling brew and a swirling crystal ball.

### What's inside (38 pieces)

**Market and harvest:** **Jack-o'-Lantern**, **Pumpkin Lamp Post**, **Potion Stall**, **Halloween Arch**, **Welcome Signpost**, **Bat Bunting**, **Pumpkin Pile**, **Pumpkin Cart**, **Gourd Basket**, **Hay Bale Seat**, **Haystack**, **Caramel Apple Tray**, **Cider Cask**, **Trick-or-Treat Bucket**, **Harvest Wreath**, **Scarecrow**

**Witch's corner:** **Witch's Cauldron**, **Spellbook Lectern**, **Crystal Ball**, **Potion Shelf**, **Potion Trio**, **Witch's Broom**, **Witch Hat Stand**, **Candle Cluster**, **Cushion Stool**

**Graveyard:** **Tombstone**, **Cross Tombstone**, **Graveyard Fence**, **Coffin**, **Ghost Lantern**, **Skull Candle**, **Glowshroom Ring**, **Lantern Tree**

**Critters:** **Black Cat**, **Owl Perch**, **Crow Post**, **Hanging Spider**, **Corner Cobweb**

### Features

- 38 pieces, 12 of them animated
- 4 lights you switch on and off (jack-o'-lantern, cauldron, lamp post, ghost lantern), plus 11 more pieces that glow
- Seats on the hay bale and the cushion stool
- Storage in the trick-or-treat bucket (9 slots), potion shelf (18) and coffin (27)
- 4 dyeable pieces: the witch hat, black cat, cushion stool and bat bunting
- Sneak + right-click the potion stall to change its awning (3 colours)
- Click sounds on the cauldron, scarecrow, cat, owl, crow, crystal ball, spellbook, cider cask and caramel apples
- Drag and Drop Easy install

### Requirements

- Minecraft **1.21.4 to 1.26.2**
- **Nexo**
