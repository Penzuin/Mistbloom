## Description

**Night Wings** is a set of six animated wing cosmetics from Mistbloom Studios.

They're worn in the backpack slot with **HMCCosmetics**. Each pair has a few small 3D pieces like claws, crystals or a gold clasp, so they don't look flat from the side.

### What's inside (6 wings)

- **Starfeather Wings:** soft pearl feathers with gold trim and a gold clasp with a moon gem. You can dye them and the trim stays gold.
- **Duskbat Wings:** bat wings with torn edges, wrist claws and a red glow near the body.
- **Frostlight Wings:** wings made of ice crystals, with a shine that moves from crystal to crystal.
- **Bloomfairy Wings:** see-through fairy wings that shift from pink to teal, with gold eyespots.
- **Nightwyrm Wings:** black dragon wings with glowing green veins, claws and a spiked spine plate.
- **Mistbloom Petal Wings:** violet and pink flower petals around a glowing bud.

### Features

- 6 animated wings
- Starfeather is dyeable
- A wings menu and a permission for each pair
- Drag and Drop Easy install

### Requirements

- Minecraft **1.21.4 to 1.26.2**
- HMCCosmetics
- **Nexo** (ItemsAdder and Oraxen folders are included too)
