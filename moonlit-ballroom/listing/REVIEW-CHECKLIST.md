# Moonlit Ballroom: review before publishing

## In this zip
- `Moonlit-Ballroom-Starter-v0.1.zip`: **what buyers download.** It holds the 6 pieces, README and license, and includes the Oct 15 fixes (stepped lantern cap, piano music rest, symmetric gilt frame, matched wisp hem, no winking).
- `Listing/`: store icon, cover, pieces sheet, waltz GIF and title art
- `LISTING.md`: store description and fields, ready to paste
- The trailer isn't in here because it's too big. It's at `Videos\Moonlit-Ballroom-Ad.mp4` on your PC.

## Test on a clean server (Paper 1.21.4+, only Nexo installed)
- [ ] Unzip the buyer zip, follow its README, then run `/nexo reload all`. Check there are no errors in the console.
- [ ] All 6 pieces show up in `/nexo inventory` with the right names and icons
- [ ] Each piece faces you when placed (the portrait isn't flipped, and nothing faces the wall)
- [ ] Ghost Pianist: you can't walk through either half of the piano, and its barrier doesn't block more than it should
- [ ] Right-clicking the Waltzing Ghosts plays a soft chime, and the Ghost Pianist plays a harp note
- [ ] The candelabra and lantern turn their light on and off, and the light really glows at night
- [ ] Animations play smoothly (the waltz, the notes, the wisp blinking, the candle flicker)
- [ ] Breaking a piece drops the piece back
