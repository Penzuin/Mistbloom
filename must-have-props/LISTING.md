## Description

**Must-Have Props** is a set of 14 everyday props from Mistbloom Studios. Lights, town pieces and storage you'll use in almost every build.

The look is plain oak, spruce, iron and burlap, so it fits medieval towns, villages, farms and spawn builds alike.

It runs on **Nexo**. Every light turns on and off, the crate and barrel open real storage, and the bench seats two.

### What's inside (14 pieces)

#### Lights
- **Street Lamp:** a two-block iron lamp post on a stone footing, with a candle lantern on top.
- **Iron Lantern:** a dark iron lantern with a candle inside and a carry ring on top.
- **Hanging Lantern:** the same lantern on a short iron chain. It hangs from the ceiling.
- **Candle Cluster:** three cream candles of different heights on a round iron dish.

#### Outdoor & town
- **Signpost:** a spruce post with two arrow boards pointing different ways.
- **Notice Board:** a standing spruce board with a little roof and paper notes pinned to it.
- **Park Bench:** a two-block bench with spruce slats on dark iron ends. Two players can sit on it.
- **Wheelbarrow:** a wooden wheelbarrow full of hay, with an iron-rimmed wheel.

#### Storage & clutter
- **Wooden Crate:** an oak crate with corner posts and a braced side. Right-click it to open 27 storage slots.
- **Crate Stack:** two crates side by side with a smaller spruce crate on top.
- **Oak Barrel:** a round oak barrel with iron hoops and a plank lid. Right-click it to open 27 storage slots.
- **Grain Sacks:** three burlap sacks tied with twine, one leaning on the others.
- **Apple Basket:** a wicker basket heaped with red apples.
- **Book Pile:** a stack of five books in red, blue, green and brown.

### Features

- 14 pieces
- 4 animated lights with flickering candle flames
- 4 working light toggles
- Storage: 27-slot crate and 27-slot barrel
- Bench seats two
- Drag and Drop Easy install

### Requirements

- Minecraft **1.21.4 to 1.26.2**
- **Nexo**
